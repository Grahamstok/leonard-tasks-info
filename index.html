AGENT LEONARD

Leonard Tasks Gmail

A little more organised.

A personal desktop task assistant that brings selected email requests and Google Tasks into one useful task list.

Why it connects to Google

The app reads selected school, sport and household emails to identify useful actions. It also imports Google Tasks, keeping their list names, notes and due dates. Saved preferences help filter irrelevant email notices.

Google access is read-only: Leonard cannot send, change or delete email, or edit your Google Tasks. Changes made in Leonard stay in Leonard.

Personal use

This is an owner's personal application, running on their own computer. This website provides information about that application. It has no sign-in, task list or mailbox access.

Privacy

Relevant email and task text may be processed by OpenAI for the task assistant. The privacy notice explains what is read, stored and shared, and how to disconnect.
