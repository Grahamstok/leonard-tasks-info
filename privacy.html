Privacy notice

Leonard Tasks Gmail · 21 October 2026

Leonard Tasks Gmail is a personal desktop application maintained by Grahamstok for the account owner's own use. This notice describes its Google email and task connection.

What the app reads

With the owner's consent, the app uses Gmail read-only access to verify the selected mailbox and read message metadata and selected message content. Its configured intake focuses on recent school, sport and household notices; financial, security and medical notices are excluded by the intake filters. Google grants mailbox-wide read permission; these narrower filters are enforced by the app.

Google Tasks read-only access reads task lists, titles, notes, due dates, hierarchy and completion/deletion changes, so the local task list can stay up to date. The app cannot send or alter email, or write changes back to Google Tasks.

How the data is used and shared

The app creates and maintains local tasks, source references, saved preferences and reversible change history. Selected email text and relevant saved task context may be sent to OpenAI through the owner's authorised ChatGPT-plan connection to interpret tasks, requests and preferences. Direct Google Tasks synchronisation does not call an AI model.

Leonard does not use Google user data to train or improve generalised AI models, sell it, use it for advertising or provide it to data brokers. Processing is limited to the personal task-management features requested by the owner. OpenAI's handling of submitted content is governed by the owner's OpenAI account settings and applicable service terms.

The local records are stored in an owner-selected folder. If that folder is synchronised or backed up by OneDrive or another service, that service may also store copies. The app does not publish tasks or emails on this information website.

Storage and protection

The app keeps imported task details, necessary email excerpts/source evidence, preferences and change history in its local database until removed by the owner. It does not intentionally keep a full mailbox archive. Sign-in and renewal credentials are encrypted for the current Windows user using Windows Data Protection API and are excluded from task exports. These protections do not encrypt the entire task database or replace the owner's computer and backup security.

Your controls

The owner can pause email and Google Tasks checks, correct or set aside local tasks, undo supported imports and export records. Deleting a task to the local bin is recoverable and does not permanently erase its history. Disconnecting the Google connection removes its local tokens and attempts to revoke Google's grant; access can also be revoked in Google Account connections.

Disconnecting does not delete previously imported records. To erase retained information, the owner must remove the local application records and any exports or backup copies they control. No Google originals are deleted.

Google API data policy

Leonard Tasks Gmail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website and contact

This static information site has no forms, advertising, analytics scripts or application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary connection data under GitHub's privacy statement. App-support and privacy questions can be raised with Grahamstok through the information repository; do not include private email, task content or credentials in public issues.

This notice will be updated when the app's data practices change.
